## Voice card in one line

Plain, deliberate, product-specific.

## Copy rules

- Headlines say what the product does for whom
- No exclamation marks in interface text
- No greeting headlines
- Short labels
- Periods only on paragraphs of several sentences
- No hype words (supercharge, seamless, unlock, magic, revolutionize, game-changing)
- Mock data looks real with plausible numbers and names
