# Copy

Copy rules for tasteskill pages.

## Headline

Names the category and the buyer or the place. One hard fact on the first screen. A noun in every heading. Buttons as verb and object.

## What may be assumed

On a place: plausible hours and prices, each marked `data-assumed`. A coined brand name (searched first, result kept in `names.json`).

## Voice

Plain verbs, sentence case, no filler. Active voice. Short labels. No exclamation marks. No greeting headlines. No hype words.
